**2. Accuracy.** Course, section, room, instructor, and slot information may be incomplete, delayed, or wrong. Always verify against ISMIS before enrolling. Generated schedules and rankings are suggestions only.

**3. Signing in to ISMIS.** To fetch the offered courses you ask for, USChed asks for your ISMIS username and password. If you provide them, you authorize USChed to use them to sign in to ISMIS on your behalf **once**, and to then run read-only searches of the offered courses for the course codes you enter. USChed will not enroll, drop, or change anything in your account, and will not read your grades, personal records, or enrollment history.

**4. What we do with your password and sign-in session.** Your password is sent over an encrypted connection, used only for that one sign-in, and then discarded. We do not save, log, or share it. So that you do not have to sign in for every search, USChed keeps the resulting ISMIS **sign-in session** (not your password) in the server's memory. It is never written to disk or a database. It ends when you sign out, after 20 minutes without activity, after 60 minutes in total, or when the server restarts. **While it is active, USChed's server holds an ISMIS session that belongs to you; USChed only uses it for the read-only course searches you request.**
